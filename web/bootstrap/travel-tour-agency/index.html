The house

A tour company that still writes the days by hand.

Maya Poluru started Vespera after a decade of leading groups that were too large to sit at one table. The list is short on purpose.

We book inns, kitchens, and walking days — not bus lots. Kavya Poluru builds the culinary routes. Arjun Poluru takes the mountains. Anika Poluru holds the desk in Austin and the WhatsApp thread on the road.

Seats open nine months out. If you need a private departure, tell Anika Poluru when you write. She will say yes only if a guide is actually free.

Itineraries

How the days actually run

Three sample routes. Meals and inns are confirmed 60 days out.

8 days · Cultural

Kyoto in late light

Maya Poluru. Gion inns, temple mornings, one free afternoon, kaiseki on night seven.

  • Guide: Maya Poluru
  • Next: 12 April · 4 October
  • $4,280 per person
  1. 01
    Gion, arrival tea

    Kansai airport transfer. Walk the lantern streets. Light supper at the inn.

  2. 02
    Fushimi at dusk

    Morning at a neighborhood shrine. Afternoon free. Torii gates after the crowds leave.

  3. 03
    Arashiyama

    Bamboo path early. River lunch. A quiet craft workshop Maya Poluru still attends.

  4. 04
    Nara day

    Train out, park walk, tofu lunch, back before evening.

  5. 05
    Tea houses

    A short ceremony, then a long conversation. Night in Gion.

  6. 06
    Open day

    Keep the inn, or take a day trip. Anika Poluru’s desk has the notes.

  7. 07
    Kaiseki

    Maya Poluru’s held table. Twelve seats. No substitutions speech — just tell us.

  8. 08
    Depart

    Breakfast, then transfers. Notes sent home the same week.

7 days · Culinary

Lisbon & the Alentejo

Kavya Poluru. Hills, pastry, and two nights in Évora for the wine.

  • Guide: Kavya Poluru
  • Next: 9 May · 3 October
  • $3,640 per person
  1. 01
    Alfama arrival

    River light, a tiled inn, and a walk that is not a hop-on bus.

  2. 02
    Belém & the market

    Pastry before the line. Then Kavya Poluru’s market lunch.

  3. 03
    Sintra, short day

    One palace, a forest walk, back for a late table.

  4. 04
    Road to Évora

    Alentejo cork and a small hotel inside the walls.

  5. 05
    Wine day

    Two estates, one long lunch. No more than ten glasses among twelve people.

  6. 06
    Return to Lisbon

    Free afternoon. Fado only if you ask — we will not insist.

  7. 07
    Depart

    Airport transfers. Kavya Poluru sends the recipes you actually cooked.

8 days · Adventure

Atlas & Marrakech

  • Next: 18 March · 8 November

On the trip

What the fare already covers

Inns, most meals, local guides, and the van. Airfare is yours.

Stays

Walkable inns, riads, and one guesthouse night on adventure routes.

Most meals

Breakfast daily, six dinners, and the kitchen days on culinary trips.

Local guides

Maya, Kavya, or Arjun Poluru plus a city specialist on the ground.

Transfers

Airport runs and the van. No leftover seats sold to a second operator.

Austin desk
