About Ashbury

A static school administration dashboard for principals and registrars. Brand color is #FB6C00. Type is Open Sans with Roboto titles.

Ashbury Academy is a fictional campus used to show enrollment, faculty, the daily schedule, attendance, and term reports. Subbu Poluru is signed in as principal. Maya Poluru runs the registrar desk. Kavya, Arjun, Anika, Nikhil, Rohan, and Priya Poluru teach or counsel.

The demo stores nothing on a server. Forms toast in the browser so you can see the flow before you wire a student information system.
